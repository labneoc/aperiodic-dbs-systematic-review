## Deduplication Note

Automated deduplication via Parsif.al reduced 243 records to 139. One additional record was removed manually before screening:

> Belova et al. "Voluntary movements cause beta oscillations increase and broadband slope decrease in the subthalamic nucleus of parkinsonian patients."
> *Eur J Neurosci.* 2021;53(7):2205–2213. doi: 10.1111/ejn.14715. Epub 2020 Mar 20.

PubMed's date filter operates on the journal issue date (2021), not the epub date (March 2020). The article was made publicly available outside the 2021–2026 eligibility window and was excluded manually. 

Final screened count: **138 records**.
