## Notes

- Page numbers are not included; items are located by section name, as final
  pagination is determined by journal typesetting.
- Item 8: screening was carried out by one reviewer; extraction and quality
  appraisal were verified against the source articles by two further reviewers,
  each covering half of the corpus. This is stated in the manuscript and listed
  among the limitations.
- Items 14, 15, 21 and 22: no assessment of reporting bias and no formal
  certainty rating (e.g. GRADE) were performed. These are recorded as not done
  rather than left blank.
- Item 24a: registered on PROSPERO (CRD420261501429).
  https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/PROSPERO/view/CRD420261501429
  Registration followed the database searches and is not prospective.
- Item 27: references this repository, including `analysis/` for the simulation code.
