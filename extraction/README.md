# extraction/

This folder contains the data extraction spreadsheet, the extraction protocol, and the adapted Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS) criteria used for quality assessment.

---

## Files

| File | Contents |
|------|----------|
| `extraction_data.xlsx` | Full extraction table (36 columns, 34 records) with NOS scores |
| `extraction_protocol.md` | Definition of all extracted fields, organized by category |
| `nos_criteria.md` | Adapted NOS criteria (7 items across 3 domains) with operational definitions |

---

## Cohort Overlap

Two records share the same patient cohort:

- **ID 09** — Joshi et al. (2025), doi: 10.1016/j.clinph.2025.03.018
- **ID 11** — Joshi et al. (2026), doi: 10.1111/ejn.70388

Both were retained as separate extraction units because they address distinct research questions. Their shared 9 patients are counted once in any aggregate N reported in the manuscript.

A second overlap exists between the Oxford sub-dataset in Gerster et al. (ID 04) and Wiest et al. 2023b (ID 24); shared patients are likewise counted once.
