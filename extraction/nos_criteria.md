# Adapted Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS) Criteria

The methodological quality and risk of bias of the included non-randomized studies were assessed using the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS), cohort version, adapted to reflect the specific methodological demands of electrophysiological DBS research. Scores range from 0 to 9 stars, classified as low (0–3), moderate (4–6), or high (7–9) quality.

No consensus quality-appraisal tool exists for aperiodic neurophysiological studies. The NOS three-domain structure was preferred for its flexibility to accommodate intraoperative, chronic-sensing, and cross-species designs.

---

## Domain 1 — Selection (max ★★★★)

| Star | Criterion | Operational Definition |
|------|-----------|------------------------|
| ★1 | **Cohort characterization** | Sample size per condition, age (mean ± SD or range), sex ratio, and disease duration explicitly reported in the manuscript body or tables. |
| ★2 | **Electrode localization** | Contact position confirmed via postoperative CT/MRI fusion (e.g., Lead-DBS pipeline), intraoperative MER trajectory with atlas-based coordinates, or validated atlas-based reconstruction. Reliance on surgical targeting coordinates alone without postoperative imaging verification does not satisfy this criterion. |
| ★3 | **Aperiodic decomposition validity** | A validated spectral parameterization method applied (FOOOF/specparam, IRASA, or equivalent). Fit quality reported (R², mean absolute error, or equivalent). Studies using non-spectral aperiodic methods (e.g., avalanche transition matrices) to which spectral fit quality does not apply are coded N/A for this criterion. |
| ★4 | **Baseline recording rigor** | An explicit resting-state or task-free baseline defined with documented artifact rejection. Visual inspection alone without a named protocol or rejection criterion does not satisfy this criterion. |

---

## Domain 2 — Comparability (max ★★)

| Star | Criterion | Operational Definition |
|------|-----------|------------------------|
| ★1 | **Primary state control** | Rigorous control of the dominant neurophysiological modulator appropriate to the study design: levodopa ON/OFF with documented washout (≥8–12 h) for PD studies; defined anesthesia protocol for intraoperative studies; ictal vs. interictal contrast for epilepsy; equivalent for other indications. A single-state recording without within-subject state manipulation does not satisfy this criterion. |
| ★2 | **Stimulation state or task standardization** | Explicit DBS ON/OFF comparison under matched conditions, or a standardized behavioral task with consistent timing across participants. Post-hoc group comparisons of stimulation parameters do not satisfy this criterion. |

---

## Domain 3 — Outcome (max ★★★)

| Star | Criterion | Operational Definition |
|------|-----------|------------------------|
| ★1 | **Validated clinical assessment** | A gold-standard, disease-specific scale used: MDS-UPDRS-III, HAMD-17, BFMDRS, TWSTRS, JRS, seizure frequency counts for epilepsy, or a psychometrically validated equivalent. Device-based surrogate scores (e.g., Kinesia One) without UPDRS as the primary longitudinal outcome do not satisfy this criterion. |
| ★2 | **Temporal adequacy** | The follow-up or recording window is appropriate to the claim: ≥3 months for neuroplastic or longitudinal claims; short-term framing explicitly stated for intraoperative or acute studies. Studies making long-term claims with only short-term data do not satisfy this criterion. |
| ★3 | **Data transparency** | Sample size per condition, artifact rejection rate (or explicit justification for its absence), and exclusion criteria explicitly reported in the manuscript. Data available only upon private request without any public repository deposition does not satisfy this criterion when combined with missing rejection rates or exclusion criteria. |

---

## Compound Criteria Note

Three criteria are compound in that a star is lost on any failing sub-component:

- **★3 (Aperiodic decomposition validity):** covers both method validation and fit quality reporting. A study using a validated method but reporting no fit quality metric does not earn this star.
- **★3 (Data transparency):** covers sample size per condition, artifact rejection rate, and exclusion criteria. Missing any one of these three elements is sufficient to withhold the star.
- **★1 (Primary state control) in Comparability:** covers washout adequacy alongside other elements of state control. A study with overnight withdrawal but no ON/OFF contrast (or equivalent manipulation) does not earn this star.

Section 3.3 of the manuscript (Sources of Methodological Heterogeneity) reports fit quality and artifact rejection at finer resolution than the compound NOS stars allow, disaggregating these practices into their own counts across all 34 studies.
